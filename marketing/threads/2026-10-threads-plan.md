## How to post (every time)
1. Post when you can stay for 60–90 minutes (evening, ~19:00–21:00 WIB is a good start).
2. One topic tag only. One image.
3. No link in the post itself — put it in your **own first reply** (test: later try one post with the link inside and compare).
4. Reply to every comment with a real sentence + a small question back. Never "makasih kak 🙏" only.
5. 2–4 posts a week, never more than 2 a day. Spend more time replying than posting.
6. Never ask for "komen YA" / "like kalau setuju" — Threads demotes it.
